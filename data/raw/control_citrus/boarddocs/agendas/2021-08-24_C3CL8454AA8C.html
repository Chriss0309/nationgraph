Recommended Action
Approve the new job description for Master Tradesworker - Roofing

Master Tradesworker- Roofing is a new job description. The Master Tradesworker-Roofing will report to the Supervisor of Facilities, Construction, and Maintenance. Performance responsibilities include install, maintain and repair various roofing types, including built-up asphalt, modified bitumen, hot-mopped and torch applied; single-ply membranes, metal roofing, asphalt, fiberglass and wood shingles and tile and install, maintain and repair flashings, expansion joints, sheet metal counter flashing, fascia and soffit, gutters and downspouts, roof penetrations for conduits, pipes, vents and curbs for equipment and inspect and determine locations and causes of leaks and repair in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions. We currently have a position funded that is not filled. Therefore, there will not be any additional cost to the budget.
