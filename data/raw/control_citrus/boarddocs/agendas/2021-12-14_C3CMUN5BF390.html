Recommended Action
Adopt Resolution Modifying the Boundaries of the Member Residence Districts of the School Board of Citrus County, Florida

 

The 2010 Census reflected a countywide population of 141,236. The new 2020 Census shows a Countywide population of 153,843, an increase of 12,607 residents. In order to balance the School Board Member Residence Areas with the County population, staff has restructured the 5 School Board member residence areas to reflect 5 districts of contiguous territory as nearly equal in population as practical.  Adjustments have been made to smooth boundaries as practical.

 

 

In order to retain common boundaries with the Board of County Commissioners, staff recommends adopting the proposed boundaries as prepared by County staff in consultation with the Department of Planning and Growth Management staff.  The School Board is not obligated to adopt the same district boundaries as the Board of County Commissioners. However, this is desirable for the Supervisor of Elections Office, and it does not present any issues for the School Board.

 

 

The proposed boundaries preserve neighborhoods, avoid splitting cities, recognize significant natural and man-made boundaries, and maintain unification with the Board of County Commissioner member election boundaries.

 

 

Pursuant to Ch. 1001.36 F.S., the School Board may make any change that it deems necessary in the boundaries of any district school board member residence area at any meeting of the district school board, provided that such changes shall be made only in odd-numbered years and that no change affect the residence qualifications of any incumbent member.

 

 

The proposed modifications seek to balance the population in line with changes that have occurred over the last decade and the proposal, and amendment process, is consistent with the provisions of Ch. 1001.36, F. S.-District School Board Member Residence Areas.

 

 

Planning and Growth Management presented the Map of the new School Board Member Residence Areas/ Election Boundaries for 2021 at the November 9, 2021, Regular School Board meeting and provided an overview of the adoption process.

 

 

The Board of County Commissioners received a similar presentation from their staff on November 9, 2021 in order to approve the districts and direct staff to prepare the necessary legal descriptions for the resolutions.

 

 

The final Resolution for the new School Board Member Residence Districts is attached for approval by the School Board.
