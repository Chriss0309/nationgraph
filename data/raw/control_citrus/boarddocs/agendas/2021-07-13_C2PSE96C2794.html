Subject
A. CCSB Withdrawal from Betz Farm Development of Regional Impact (DRI) and request to redesignate CCSB Owned Tracts to “Public Semi-Public Institutional” (PSI) Land Use on the Citrus County Comprehensive Plan Generalized Future Land Use Map and Land Development Code Atlas
Meeting
Jul 13, 2021 - REGULAR SCHOOL BOARD MEETING - Amended
Category
VI. SCHOOL SUPPORT SERVICES, JONNY BISHOP - Planning and Growth Management, Chuck Dixon - CONSENT
Type
Information

 

The Betz Farm Development of Regional Impact (DRI) is jointly owned by the Citrus County Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) and the Citrus County School Board with 70 Percent owned by the County and 30 Percent owned by the School Board.

 

The School Board portion of the Betz Farm Development of Regional Impact (DRI) has been mostly developed and is currently being used for educational purposes as part of the Crystal River High School Campus. The BOCC portion is undeveloped, designated for residential development, and the BOCC has indicated a desire to sell it for development.

 

The maintenance of the DRI Annual Reporting Process has worked well since the acquisition of the property by the BOCC and the School Board in 2002, because all the Tracts have remained in public ownership. However, with the majority owner of Tract “A”, (currently owned by the BOCC), potentially shifting to private ownership, it is preferred that the School Board Portion (Tracts “B” and “C”) be removed from the DRI and zoned accordingly for a public use.

 

The letter signed by Superintendent Himmel serves as a formal request to initiate the processes to:

 

1.  Remove the School Board Tracts from the Betz Farm Development of Regional Impact Development Order, and, 

 

2.  Designate the School Board Tracts using the appropriate land use category in the County Comprehensive Plan and on Land Development Code Zoning Atlas.

 

 

This clarifies that the land will be used for a public purpose as opposed to other land uses.

 

 

This is preferred in the event that the County owned Tract is sold for development.

 

 

It is also beneficial in the event that the School Board owned Tracts are annexed into the City of Crystal River in the future.  The remainder of the Crystal River High School Campus is already located within the City of Crystal River. 

 

Please contact me if you have any questions or need further clarification.

The following is a summary of the necessary property acquisitions and the associated license agreement and lease agreement that are necessary for the Florida Department of Transportation to move forward with the US-41 Road Widening Project adjacent to Inverness Middle School:

 

1. Parcels 124A and 124B – fee simple purchase

a. Compensation Total: $51,000

b. Total Parcel 124A land area is 2,482 square feet and Parcel 124B is 539 square feet.

c. Improvements in the area are asphalt paving, concrete spillway, pavement markings, signs, 5 foot high chain link fencing, and sod.

d. Per the request of the School Board, the FDOT has agreed to install temporary fencing to replace the fencing in the area of Parcel 124 during construction.  The temporary fencing will be 5 feet high, galvanized chain link fencing similar to what is currently in place along US 41.

e. Compensation has been included in the purchase amount for the School Board to install permanent fencing once the project is complete.

 

2. Parcel 700 – temporary (5 year) construction easement

a. Compensation Total: $15,300

b. An irregular shape measuring 7,347 square feet.

c. Improvements in the area are asphalt paving, concrete spillway, pavement markings, signs, 5 foot high chain link fencing, and sod.

d. Purpose – realignment of Middle School Road, drainage installation, and related construction activities.

 

3. Parcel 900 – license agreement

a. This parcel was added to accommodate the request from the School Board that the FDOT install temporary fencing for the duration of construction.

b. Allows FDOT access to install the fencing.

 

4. Lease Agreement for School Sign

a. Sign currently exists in the Right of Way and may remain in the Right of Way during / after the road project.

b. 25-year $0 rent lease, with an option to renew for an additional 25 years.

 

Summary:

 

The total compensation is $66,300 total for parcels 124A and 124B and Parcel 700 (temporary construction easement). The Florida Department of Transportation will install temporary fencing during the project and the fencing will be left in place and can be used / relocated by the School Board after the project is complate.  The IMS School sign may remain in place on State Right of Way for 25-50 years at no cost to the School Board.

 

 

Parcels 124A and B have already been deemed not necessary for educational purposes by the Board at the Regular Meeting on April 13, 2021 and the Spot Survey was approved by the Department of Education on April 14, 2021.

 

The FDOT projects that demolition of structures in the ROW Project Area will begin in January 2023 and the construction will begin between December of 2022 and June of 2023.
