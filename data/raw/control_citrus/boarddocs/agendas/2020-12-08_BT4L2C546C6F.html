Approve/Adopt Policy 5.203 COVID-19 Temporary Student Assignment to Citrus Virtual School While Assigned to an Out of Zone School

This is a temporary new policy created for students attending Citrus Virtual School while on a zone waiver. It was presented at the July 14, 2020, School Board Meeting and approved to move forward for Request to Advertise. 

 

It is not technically necessary for blended learning when students are enrolled directly in their home brick and mortar school but it does provide for enrollment flexibility while reassuring students and families that they can remain at their school of choice and graduate if eligible even while attending via CVS during the Pandemic.

Approve Impact Fee Report to Transmit to the Board of County Commissioners for review and implementation

The Impact Fee Schedule must be updated every few years in order to accurately reflect current conditions including student generation rates and the costs associated with growth related capital expenses.  The Impact Fee Schedule is based on a report which must be updated and populated with current data.

 

Following approval by the School Board, the report will be transmitted to the County for review by the Board of County Commissioners as part of the overall impact fee program.

 

A recommendation may accompany the report transmittal if the Board chooses to add any clarification or specific instructions for consideration.

 

Staff recommends Approval of the Impact Fee Update Report and transmittal to the Board of County Commissioners.
