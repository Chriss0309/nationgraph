Perkins V Career and Technical Education Through Entrepreneurship Education and Training Grant

Entrepreneurship education and training (EET) refers to both academic education and formal training interventions or activities that share the broad objective of providing individuals with the knowledge and skills necessary to start, operate, or scale an enterprise. For purposes of Perkins V, it has a particular focus on formally cultivating within CTE students the following two domains: entrepreneurial mindsets and entrepreneurial capabilities. Alachua County School District is requesting $50,000 for two Entrepreneurial initiatives at Santa Fe High School's Academy of Agriscience and Academy of Veterinary Assisting. This is a competitive grant.

14. Interlocal Agreement between the City of Gainesville and the School Board of Alachua County for Public Use of Howard Bishop Middle School Recreation Facilities (Andrew)
Meeting
Sep 21, 2021 - School Board of Alachua County, Florida
Category
C. Consent Agenda
Type
Action (Consent)

Florida Statute, Chapter 163, authorizes public agencies, including cities and school boards to enter into agreements to provide services and to jointly exercise the power, privilege, or authority they share in common. 
The City of Gainesville and the School Board of Alachua County wish to coordinate the recreational facilities and activities of the City of Gainesville and the School Board of Alachua County in order to prevent duplication and provide more fully for the recreational needs of the neighbors of our City and the School Board of Alachua County at the least expense.
The City of Gainesville has the experience, expertise, staff and abilities to support the use of the School Board recreational facilities for public use during non-school hours.
The City of Gainesville agrees to provide $250,000 in funding from Wild Spaces and Public Places as part of the planned school improvement project in return for the School Board of Alachua County providing public access to said recreational facilities outside normal school hours.
