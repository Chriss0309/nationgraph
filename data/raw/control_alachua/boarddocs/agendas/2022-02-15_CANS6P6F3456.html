Subject
5. American Rescue Plan - Homeless Children and Youth (ARP-HCY) Project; Florida McKinney-Vento Program (FMVP)(Bokor)
Meeting
Feb 15, 2022 - School Board of Alachua County, Florida
Category
D. Consent Agenda
Type
Action (Consent)

As directed by the request for applications, the purposes of these funds are to identify homeless children and youth (HCY), provide HCY with wraparound services to address the challenges of COVID-19, and enable HCY to attend school and fully participate in school activities. This is a discretionary non-competitive award for the budget period December 1, 2021 – September 30, 2023. ACPS's budget request is $493,340.00.

 

 

Goal

Justification

Timeline

1

Increase homeless student identification rate (HSIR) in charter schools and district schools from 6% to 10% by June 30, 2024

As of summer 2021, the district HSIR stands at 6% as compared the desired target of 10% HSIR. Providing a para to oversee data tracking and input of Homeless status of students in the SIS system as well as coordinating emergency and essential items for them should improve the HSIR rate by June 2024.

June 30, 2024

2

Increase homeless student attendance rate (HSAR) in elementary, middle and high school from 84% to 91% by June 30,2024

Currently, the district HSAR stands at 84.3% as compared to non-homeless peers at 91.05%.  Providing 2 additional Dropout Prevention Specialists to address attendance at the high school and elementary school levels should improve the HSAR rate to be closer to the non-homeless peer AR.  In addition, providing targeted mental health services to elementary and high school students should improve their attendance also.

June 30,2024

3

Increase homeless student promotion rate (HSPR) from 91% to 95% by June 30, 2024

As of 2021, the district HSPR was 91.01% as compared to the non-homeless promotion rate of 95.81%.  Providing a Mentoring & Tutoring Coordinator and mentoring & tutoring services should improve the HSPR to be more commensurate to non-homeless peer promotion rates.

June 30, 2024

6. Amendment of the District Advisory Council Bylaws (Simon)
Meeting
Feb 15, 2022 - School Board of Alachua County, Florida
Category
E. Action Items
Type
Action
Recommended Action
The Superintendent recommends that the Board approve the amendments of the DAC Bylaws, as presented.

In November 2013, the School Board of Alachua County established the District Advisory Council (DAC) to serve as a vehicle to provide stakeholder/community input on key issues of districtwide significance.

 

During the Fall 2021 semester, the Superintendent sought insight from the DAC on their experience engaging with the District. The DAC considered ways to develop a committee structure that improves communication, increases member engagement, and fosters more meaningful community input.  

 

Based on the feedback of DAC members, the Superintendent developed amendments to the DAC Bylaws for consideration. At their meeting on January 31, 2022, the DAC voted unanimously to adopt the amendments to the DAC Bylaws and refer them to the School Board for approval.
