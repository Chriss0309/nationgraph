There are three ways citizens may offer their input for this meeting: 1) submit input through email at issues@gm.sbac.edu by noon the day of the board meeting; and 2) go to the Board Room to offer their input in person during the meeting; or 3) Join by phone +1 352 955 7646, phone call will be placed in a queue, name and information will be taken before call is transferred to the board.

Persons with disabilities who require assistance to participate in the meeting are requested to notify the Office of the Superintendent at (352) 955-7527 (Voice) or dial 711 (TDD) at least 48 hours in advance, so their needs can be accommodated.
