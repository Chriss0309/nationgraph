Recommended Action :
The superintendent recommends authorization be granted to advertise the proposed amendment to School Board Policy 502, "Emergency Operations."
Goals :
Guardrail 3: Resources & Operational Processes

Public Content

PRESENTED BY:

Mr. Kevin W. Pendley, General Counsel; and Mr. Mark Shanoff, Chief Operating Officer

 

BACKGROUND:  

School Board Policy 507, "Emergency Operations," adopted in 2005 provides a framework a for the mandatory report of duty for administrators while bargaining unit personnel duties during storm events are regulated by collective bargaining agreements and applicable paid overtime laws. 

 

The proposed amendment would change how administrators and non-administrative non-bargaining employees are compensated during emergency operations.  

 

Administrators activated during the operation of our shelters receive his or her regular daily rate of pay for duties during the weekend or what would have been a non-duty day, such as a district holiday if a minimum of 8 hours are worked. A shorter work day would result in the hourly rate of pay. During a regular work week, administrators currently receive an additional $175 per day if a minimum of eight consecutive hours is worked or four consecutive hours worked on the day the shelter ceases operations. 

 

Under the revised policy, administrators and staff members will be paid based on a daily/hourly rate of pay for all hours worked, with hours over 40 worked during one-week period paid at time and one-half.
