BACKGROUND:  

All vacant positions outside the classroom and not directly related to student health and safety are subject to intensive review prior to being filled. Teachers noted out of field in "ESOL" are in field in their subject area but have received an English Language Learner Student which triggers the requirement for English Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) training. Teachers are immediately notified of this requirement by the District Certification Department. This training is offered throughout the year within the Volusia County Schools Professional Development program. These teachers are considered out of field until they have fulfilled this requirement.

The superintendent recommends approval of the proposed custodial pilot program, effective July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022, and that the superintendent be authorized to take all necessary steps to implement that decision.
Goals :
Guardrail 3: Resources & Operational Processes

Public Content

PRESENTED BY:

Mr. Mark West, Chief Human Resources Officer; and Mr. John Kraft, Coordinator, Facilities Maintenance & Operations

 

BACKGROUND:  

Based on current school board and employee complaints regarding the cleanliness of their campuses/classrooms as well as failed Core inspections (with scores below 85%), VCS is proposing to pilot an in-house custodial operation at 15 selected schools in the district to compare the difference between outsourcing and in-house custodial services.

 

Hypothesis: The pilot program will show that an in-house school-based custodial staff will perform at or above the current level of service that is being provided by an ABM contractor in all inspections grades, the number of complaints received, and satisfaction grading from the school-based staff at a comparable or lesser cost to the district. Staff will compare Core inspection result scores between contractor services and the pilot schools to evaluate if there are improvements and a significant difference in the quality of services provided.

 

The program will run for one (1) school year from July 1, 2021, to June 30, 2022.

Public Content

If any person appearing before the School Board anticipates or expects that he or she might appeal any decision made by the School Board, that person will need a record of the proceedings, and that, for such purpose, he or she will need to ensure that a verbatim record of the proceedings is made, which record includes the testimony and evidence upon which the appeal is to be based. 286.0105, Florida Statutes.

 

American with Disabilities Act

In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, persons with a disability, who require accommodations to participate in this proceeding should contact the Superintendent's Office, Volusia County Schools, DeLand Administrative Complex, 200 North Clara Avenue, DeLand, FL 32721, phone 386-734-7190; or Florida Relay Service 1-800-955-8770 (v). Reasonable notice is required; at least five days notice is requested.
