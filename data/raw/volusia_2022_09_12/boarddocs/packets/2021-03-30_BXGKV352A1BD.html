2. Custodial Pilot Program
Subject :
2.01 Custodial Pilot Program Presentation
Meeting :
Mar 30, 2021 - Agenda - 1:00 PM - Workshop/Work Session - The School Board of Volusia County, Florida
Type :
Presentation
Goals :
Guardrail 3: Resources & Operational Processes

Public Content

PRESENTED BY:

Mr. Mark West, Chief Human Resources Officer; Mr. John Kraft, Coordinator, Facilities Maintenance & Operations; and Mr. Stephen Bouzianis, Consultant

 

BACKGROUND:  

Based on current school board and employee complaints regarding the cleanliness of their campuses/classrooms as well as failed Core inspections (with scores below 85%), VCS is proposing to pilot an in-house custodial operation at 15 selected schools in the district to compare the difference between outsourcing and in-house custodial services.

 

Hypothesis: The pilot program will show that an in-house school-based custodial staff will perform at or above the current level of service that is being provided by an ABM contractor in all inspections grades, number of complaints, and satisfaction grading from the school-based staff at a comparable or lesser cost to the district.

 

The program will run for one (1) school year from July 1, 2021, to June 30, 2022.

 

SUBMITTED BY:   

John Kraft, Coordinator, Facilities Maintenance & Operations
