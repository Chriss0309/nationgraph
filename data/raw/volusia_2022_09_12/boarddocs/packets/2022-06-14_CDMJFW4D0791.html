Public Content

BACKGROUND:

Building Fact Fluency is primarily intended to serve as supplemental curriculum to support Individual Education Plan goals for students when the operations are central to the major work of their grades, i.e., K-2 for addition and subtraction, and grade 3-5 for multiplication and division. Students who, for a variety of reasons such as learning loss from Covid-19, need additional and focused support as they develop understanding of the operations and learn their basic number combinations.
