Subject :
11.02 Property Insurance Renewals: 2021-2022
Meeting :
Apr 27, 2021 - Agenda - 5:30 PM - The School Board of Volusia County, Florida
Type :
Action (Consent)
Budgeted :
Yes
Recommended Action :
The superintendent recommends negotiation and approval to purchase property insurance for the renewal period of May 1, 2021 through May 1, 2022, not to exceed $3,450,000.
Goals :
Guardrail 3: Resources & Operational Processes

Public Content

BACKGROUND:

During the last year, insured property values for the School District increased 2.4% from $1,655,745,715 to $1,694,975,135.  Factoring this growth in insured property values, the annualized cost of the current 2020-2021 property insurance in force is $3,373,726.  

 

The School District’s projected property insurance rate increase will not exceed 2%. The School District anticipates the ability to obtain this property insurance renewal with like/same terms for 2021-2022 term for its facilities. 

 

The School District’s 2% property insurance rate increase is well below industry average, especially in the Florida public entity segment, where property rate increases consistently average 10-20%.  Additionally, this insurance renewal keeps in place the existing competitive and prudent limits, deductibles, and coverage terms that have been extensively negotiated. The last five years of CAT losses due to hurricanes, earthquakes, wildfires, floods, and winter storms have pushed property insurance rates into a globally hard market. The School District has navigated this hard market exceedingly well compared to its peers. 

 

The overall budget, including the adjustment for increased property values, is an increase of $150,000 over last year’s $3,300,000.

Goals :
Guardrail 4: Communication & Community Engagement

Public Content

PRESENTED BY:

Ms. Kelly Amy, Manager, Strategic Partnerships; Mr. Erik Nason, AdventHealth Sports Medicine Outreach Coordinator; Mr. Evan Ernst, Who We Play For Director; and Mr. Lary Beal, Specialist, Athletics

 

BACKGROUND:

Sudden Cardiac Arrest (SEA) is one of the leading causes of death amongst high school athletes. In an effort to advance the current pre-participation sports physicals and to align with best practices in sports medicine, AdventHealth is recommending the addition of cardiac screening. These ECG screenings will identify cardiac abnormalities in approximately 3% of the student athlete population resulting in immediate preventive care and appropriate treatment. The goal is to safeguard the lives of student athletes harboring these cardiac abnormalities.
