Recommended Action :
District Support Services is requesting approval of the Florida Power & Light (FPL) Bill of Sale for the Photovoltaic array installed in 2015 at Vineland Elementary School, East Elementary School, Sallie Jones Elementary School and Neil Armstrong Elementary School.

Public Content

  Florida Power and Light (FPL) and The School Board of Charlotte County, Florida entered into the Photovoltaic for Schools Pilot Program Contract with above mentioned schools on March 17, 2015.

  FPL has agreed to assign, transfer and convey all of FPL's right, title, interest in and to the Photovoltaic System, to the School Board effective December 9, 2020

Public Content

Citizen input is vital to the improvement of our school district. Most times your remarks may be listened to without comment from the Board Members or Superintendent. This does not mean they are heard with any less intensity or concern. Where appropriate, the School Board, Superintendent and staff will investigate your comments and report back to you with an answer.

 

We thank you for taking the time and having the concern to make our district better.
