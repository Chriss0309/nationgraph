Comprehensive Safety Inspections were conducted at the twenty-nine (29) district facilities according to Section 1013.12, Florida Statutes, State Requirements for Education Facilities, Section 5, and Florida Administrative Code, Chapter 69A-58. Each Comprehensive Inspection included a fire safety, casualty safety, and sanitation inspection of the facility.

The inspection reports are filed with the state and local fire marshals and maintained by the Charlotte county Public Schools.
