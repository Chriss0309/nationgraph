1. Uniform Dress Renewal for Deep Creek Elementary School
Meeting :
Dec 7, 2021 - Regular School Board Meeting
Category :
F. ACTION AGENDA
Type :
Action
Recommended Action :
Approval is requested of the School Based Uniform Dress Code Policy Extension for Deep Creek Elementary.

Public Content

 

The Division of Learning and Deep Creek Elementary request School Board approval to renew School Board Policy 5511 School-Based Uniform Dress Code Policy for five years SY 2022/23-2026/27. 

 

Deep Creek Elementary enrollment for PreK - 4 is 678 families. Out of a total of 546 responses received, 426 families were in favor of extending the uniform policy. This satisfies the two-thirds requirement to present the policy and request our School-Based Uniform Dress Code Policy be renewed for five years (SY 2022/23 - SY 2026/27).
