Subject :
2. SY 2020-2021 School Bus Runs
Meeting :
Jul 24, 2020 - Regular School Board Meeting
Category :
E. ACTION AGENDA
Type :
Action
Recommended Action :
District Support Services is requesting School Board approval for the 2020-2021 school year bus runs.

Public Content

 

Board approval is requested for the school bus runs for the 2020-2021 school year.  During SY 2019 -2020, the district ended with 88 school buses for Charlotte County schools. Transportation will begin the 2020 – 2021 School Year with 90 buses. The number of buses is slightly higher due not only to the increase in the ESE student population and moving of ESE units to different schools, but also for student safety under current CDC guidelines.  There are also three (3) dedicated routes which service Florida Southwest Collegiate High School (FSW).

 

Based on current information the earliest pick up time will be approximately 5:03 a.m. for Charlotte High School (CHS) and the last drop off time will be approximately 5:59 p.m. for Punta Gorda Middle School (PGM).  The longest ride time is approximately 119 minutes in the afternoon serving PGMS.  There are approximately 50 students assigned to the route.   The shortest ride times are approximately 3 minutes serving Meadow Park Elementary School (MPE).  The runs will continue to be adjusted as new students enroll and ridership is balanced.

 

Run information may be viewed using VEO InfoShare.  School Board members may also access this information through the Internet by:

 

  1. Entering http://ms-veoi:680

 

  1. At the log in screen:  Enter your name and password.  Contact the Routing/Scheduling Manager, or the Director of Transportation to receive your individual logins and passwords.

 

This site may be bookmarked at the Logon screen for easy reference.  Transportation information may be viewed by individual school, bus number, or by student.  Use of this site will provide next day information on routes throughout the year.

 

A hard copy of all routes serving each school will be available for your review in the Superintendent’s office on Friday, July 24, 2020 by 9:30 a.m.

Motion & Voting

District Support Services is requesting School Board approval for the 2020-2021 school year bus runs.

Motion by Kim Amontree, second by Cara Reynolds.
Final Resolution: Motion carries
Yea: Kim Amontree, Wendy Atkinson, Cara Reynolds, Bob Segur, Ian Vincent
