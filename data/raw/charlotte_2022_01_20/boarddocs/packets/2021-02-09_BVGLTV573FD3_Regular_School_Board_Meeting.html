Citizen input is vital to the improvement of our school district. Most times your remarks may be listened to without comment from the Board Members or Superintendent. This does not mean they are heard with any less intensity or concern. Where appropriate, the School Board, Superintendent and staff will investigate your comments and report back to you with an answer.

 

We thank you for taking the time and having the concern to make our district better.
