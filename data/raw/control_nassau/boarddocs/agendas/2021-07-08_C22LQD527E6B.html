Meeting
Jul 8, 2021 - The School Board of Nassau County, Florida
Category
E. Consent Agenda
Type
Action (Consent)
Recommended Action
Approve employee payroll deduction slot for Roth 403B with Equitable.
Goals
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.

AGENDA STATEMENT:

Approve employee payroll deduction slot for Roth 403B with Equitable.

 

ISSUE:

Equitable offers retirement investment planning to district employees.  While employees can payroll deduct for 403B and 457 plans with Equtiable, we do not currently have employee payroll deduction for this company's Roth 403B plan.  Many employees have requested this option. 

 

ALTERNATIVES:

1. Approve the Roth 403B employee payroll deduction with Equitable.
2. Disapprove the Roth 403B employee payroll deduction with Equitable.

 

RECOMMENDATIONS:

The Superintendent recommends approval of the Roth 403B employee payroll deduction with Equitable.

 

RATIONALE:

Adding the Roth 403B employee payroll deduction would benefit our employees.

 

 

IMPACT STATEMENT:

There is no direct financial affect on schools or budgets by the approval of the Roth 403B employee payroll deduction.  Chris Lacambra

ADDRESSING THE BOARD:

Individuals or groups may address comments to the School Board at any Regular Meeting by following established procedures.  The maximum allotted time for a presentation is three (3) minutes per person.  A speaker's time may not be allocated to others.  The Board will permit individuals' comments on agenda items at the time the item is under consideration.  Individuals must complete a form located on a table near the rear wall of the Board Room.  Thirty minutes will be set aside at the end of the meeting for public discussion on non-agenda items.
