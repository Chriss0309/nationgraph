Recommended Action
Authorize the disposal of surplus property equipment for December 9, 2021.
Goals
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.
Growth Management and Operations - Strategy 1 - Create community buy-in for support of alternative revenue sources to support capital projects.

AGENDA STATEMENT: Authorize the disposal of surplus/cannibalized equipment currently valued at $759.92 for December 9, 2021.

 

ISSUE: Florida Statutes 274.01"Authorizing and recording of the disposal of property" states that the authority for the disposal of property shall be recorded in the minutes of the governmental unit.

 

ALTERNATIVES:

The Board's alternatives are:

1. Approve the request for approval of changes to Property Inventory.

2. Disapprove the request for approval of changes to Property Inventory.

 

RECOMMENDATIONS: The Superintendent recommends approval of changes to Property Inventory. 

 

RATIONALE: The property records of the School Board are maintained under the direction of the Superintendent with approval of the Board. The District's current practice for computer refresh is every five years.

 

IMPACT STATEMENT: Assets for the School District will be reduced and/or increased by the amounts stated above. The missing/stolen property items are deducted from the site balance, but will be maintained in the system to be reinstated as found.

(The School Board cannot take action on input/request(s), but may place an item on the agenda of a subsequent meeting for action).  Presentations to the Board are limited to three (3) minutes.

 

ADDRESSING THE BOARD:

Individuals or groups may address comments to the School Board at any Regular Meeting by following established procedures.  The maximum allotted time for a presentation is three (3) minutes per person.  A speaker's time may not be allocated to others.  The Board will permit individuals' comments on agenda items at the time the item is under consideration.  Individuals must complete a form located on a table near the rear wall of the Board Room.  Thirty minutes will be set aside at the end of the meeting for public discussion on non-agenda items.
