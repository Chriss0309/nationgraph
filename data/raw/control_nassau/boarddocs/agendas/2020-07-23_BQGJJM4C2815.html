12. Humana Dental and Vision Insurance Plans
Meeting
Jul 23, 2020 - The School Board of Nassau County, Florida
Category
E. Consent Agenda
Type
Action (Consent)
Recommended Action
To approve continuation of the Humana Dental and Vision Insurance Plans as presented.
Goals
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.

AGENDA STATEMENT: To approve continuation of the Humana Dental and Vision Insurance Plans: Dental Plan HS205, Dental Plan Advantage AVN2, Dental Plan PPO, and Vision Plan, and the respective plan rates. To approve the elimination of Humana Dental Plan CS150 (Current CS150 Plan members will automatically transfer to HS205 Plan without disruption of services). There is a rate increase for Dental Plan HS205 and Dental Plan PPO for 2020-2021. Humana offered a no rate increase for Dental Plan Advantage AVN2 and the Vision Plan for 2020-2021.

 

ISSUE: The Insurance Committee comprised of administrative representatives (2), NTA representatives (3), and NESPA representatives (3), along with Broker/Consultants Miller Health Group and The Bailey Group met during the course of the year. Based upon the Dental RFP, the insurance committee voted to stay with Humana and eliminate Dental Plan CS150 (Current CS150 Plan members will automatically transfer to HS205 Plan without disruption of services).  Humana offered a rate increase for Dental Plan HS205 and Dental Plan PPO for 2020-2021. Humana offered a no rate increase for Dental Plan Advantage AVN2.  Humana offered a no rate increase for the Vision Plan for 2020-2021.

 

ALTERNATIVES: Approve the plan rates. Provide recommendations.

 

 

RECOMMENDATIONS: The Superintendent recommends approval of the plan rates and changes.

 

RATIONALE: The Insurance Committee is very conscious of the cost that employees pay for dental and vision as well as the costs that employees pay for services covered by Humana. The Insurance Committee felt the rates and benefits offered by Humana was greater than the offer from Florida Blue, which included higher cost plans and disruption of services for members. The Insurance Committee opted to eliminate the CS150 Plan (Current CS150 Plan members will automatically transfer to HS205 Plan without disruption of services) and sustain an increase for the HS205 Plan and PPO Plan. 

 

IMPACT STATEMENT: There is no budget impact as employees pay the full premium for dental and vision insurance. See attached chart that contains the rates for 2020-2021.


DATA SOURCE: Christopher Lacambra, Executive Director of Business Services

ADDRESSING THE BOARD:

Individuals or groups may address comments to the School Board at any Regular Meeting by following established procedures.  The maximum allotted time for a presentation is three (3) minutes per person.  A speaker's time may not be allocated to others.  The Board will permit individuals' comments on agenda items at the time the item is under consideration.  Individuals must complete a form located on a table near the rear wall of the Board Room.  Thirty minutes will be set aside at the end of the meeting for public discussion on non-agenda items.
