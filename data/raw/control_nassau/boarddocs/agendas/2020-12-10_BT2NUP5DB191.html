IMPACT STATEMENT: Assets for the School District will be reduced and/or increased by the amounts stated above. The missing/stolen property items are deducted from the site balance, but will be maintained in the system to be reinstated as found.

1. RFP for the District Life Insurance Business
Meeting
Dec 10, 2020 - The School Board of Nassau County, Florida
Category
F. Action Items
Type
Action
Recommended Action
To take the district's life insurance business, currently with Trustmark, to RFP for the 2021-2022 school year.
Goals
Human Resources - Strategy 1 - Recruit and retain innovative teachers who will inspire students to explore academic and career options.
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.

AGENDA STATEMENT:  The Insurance Committee recommends that the board grant the Bailey Group permission to take the district's life insurance business, currently with Trustmark, to RFP for 2021-2022.

 

ISSUE: The insurance committee would like to ensure that the employees are getting competitive rates, benefits and the best customer service available. 

 

ALTERNATIVES:  Do not take the district's life insurance to RFP for 2020- 2021.

 

RECOMMENDATIONS: The Insurance Committee recommends that the board grant the Bailey Group permission to take the district's life insurance, currently with Trustmark, to RFP for 2021-2022.

 

RATIONALE: By going to RFP, we can ensure that the employees are getting the most competitive rates, benefits and the best customer service available.  This has not been done since before 1996 when the district first started using Trustmark.  Prior to 1996,  the district used the company that Trustmark purchased, called Washington Life.  We have no record of the business going to RFP during  the Washington Life era either.

 

IMPACT STATEMENT:  The Bailey Group would take the district's life insurance business to RFP to ensure that we are getting competitive rates, benefits and the best customer service.

 

DATA SOURCE:  Insurance Committee and the Bailey Group

ADDRESSING THE BOARD:

Individuals or groups may address comments to the School Board at any Regular Meeting by following established procedures.  The maximum allotted time for a presentation is three (3) minutes per person.  A speaker's time may not be allocated to others.  The Board will permit individuals' comments on agenda items at the time the item is under consideration.  Individuals must complete a form located on a table near the rear wall of the Board Room.  Thirty minutes will be set aside at the end of the meeting for public discussion on non-agenda items.
