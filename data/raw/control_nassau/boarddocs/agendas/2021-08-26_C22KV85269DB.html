Recommended Action
Approve Annual Audit of Schools' Internal Accounts for fiscal year 2020-2021
Goals
Growth Management and Operations - Strategy 1 - Create community buy-in for support of alternative revenue sources to support capital projects.
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.

AGENDA STATEMENT: Approve Annual Audit of Schools' Internal Accounts for fiscal year 2020-2021.

 

ISSUE: State Board of Administrative Rule 6A-1.087 requires the Board to provide for audits of the Schools' Internal Accounts by a certified professional accountant. The Rule also requires that audits be presented at a board meeting and filed as part of the public record.

 

ALTERNATIVES:

1. Approve the annual audit of the Schools' Internal Accounts.

2. Disapprove the annual audit of the Schools' Internal Accounts.

 

RECOMMENDATIONS: The Superintendent recommends approval of the Schools' Internal Accounts audit.

 

RATIONALE: School audits are required by the State Board of Education Rule and must be presented to the Board for approval of these audits.

 

IMPACT STATEMENT: There is no direct effect of the schools or budgets by the approval of these audits.

ADDRESSING THE BOARD:

Individuals or groups may address comments to the School Board at any Regular Meeting by following established procedures.  The maximum allotted time for a presentation is three (3) minutes per person.  A speaker's time may not be allocated to others.  The Board will permit individuals' comments on agenda items at the time the item is under consideration.  Individuals must complete a form located on a table near the rear wall of the Board Room.  Thirty minutes will be set aside at the end of the meeting for public discussion on non-agenda items.
