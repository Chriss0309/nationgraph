3. Basic and Voluntary Life Insurance Coverage
Meeting
May 13, 2021 - The School Board of Nassau County, Florida
Category
F. Action Items
Type
Action
Recommended Action
The Insurance Committee recommends that the Board approve Met Life to provide the Basic and Voluntary Life Insurance Coverage for all employees, effective January 1, 2022. Furthermore, to increase the Basic Coverage Amounts for all full-time employees to $40,000 and to implement the Plan Source Benefits Administration System with the premium savings.
Goals
Human Resources - Strategy 2 - Competitive salaries, affordable health insurance, incentives, and positive working conditions to retain quality teachers and staff within the school system.

AGENDA STATEMENT: The Insurance Committee recommends that the board approve Met Life to provide the Basic and Voluntary Life Insurance Coverage for all employees, effective January 1, 2022.  Furthermore, to increase the Basic Coverage Amounts for all full-time employees to $40,000 and to implement the Plan Source Benefits Administration System with the premium savings.   

 

ISSUE: The recent RFP that the Miller and Bailey Groups conducted on the district's  behalf indicated that we could save a substantial amount ($57,492 annually)  each year based on our current rates by switching carriers.  Met Life will also help subsidize our preferred electronic benefits platform ( Plan Source). 

 

ALTERNATIVES: Keep our current carrier, Trustmark Life, or pursue other carriers and/or electronic benefits platforms. 

 

RECOMMENDATIONS: To approve Met Life to provide the Basic and Voluntary Life Insurance Coverage for all employees, effective January 1, 2022.  Furthermore, to increase the Basic Coverage amounts for all full-time employees to $40,000 and to implement the Plan Source Benefits Administration System with the premium savings.   

 

RATIONALE: Our current rates with Trustmark are higher than available through Met-Life.  Switching to Met-Life would allow us to save approximately $57,492 annually on our Basic Life premiums, which could be used to re-invest in employee benefits and services by increasing basic coverage amounts, and adopting an electronic benefits platform.  Additionally, Met-Life has a higher guaranteed voluntary coverage amount ( $250.000 vs. $95,000). 

 

IMPACT STATEMENT: The total additional cost to increase the Basic Insurance coverage to $40.000 and implement the Plan Source Benefits Administration Platform is approximately, $3,896 annually.


DATA SOURCE: Miller/ Bailey Group Presentation and rate quotes.
