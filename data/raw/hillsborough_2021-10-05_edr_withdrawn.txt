DESCRIPTION:

Endpoint detection and response (EDR) will detect threats in your networking environment and respond to them. It can analyze the nature of the threat and give ITS information regarding how the threat was initiated, which parts of your network it has attacked, what it is currently doing, and how to stop the attack altogether. An EDR solution further protects your network by containing the threat to prevent it from spreading. EDR can protect your organization from threats, whether you use a fully in-house system or incorporate a cloud platform. The information provided is critical and aids in mitigation/remediation in the moments before and after an attack, and is an integral part the district's updated security posture.

The initial term of this agreement is from May 1, 2020 through April 30, 2023, with two (2) additional one (1) year renewal periods. This agreement may be terminated before the next year's license purchase. This contract will be reviewed and evaluated and may be renewed by the Superintendent.
